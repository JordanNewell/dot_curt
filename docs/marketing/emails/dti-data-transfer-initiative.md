Hello,

I maintain .curt, an open file format for AI chat conversations. The literature sweep behind a paper I wrote found your AI Conversation History schema (November 2025, revised May 2026) to be the nearest peer, and it earns the place: the first open specification aimed at AI conversation lock-in, from a body whose purpose is portability engineering, motivated exactly as we are — "your conversations with your AI tools and services are your personal data."

The distinction, stated precisely: as published, the registry entry carries no version number, no stated license, no validation semantics, and no conformance vectors, and its data model is a generic sender/text/channel transcript. .curt is a frozen-core spec (v1) with strict whole-file validation, an additive extension registry, and 27 executable conformance vectors run in CI, MIT throughout.
